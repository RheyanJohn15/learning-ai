## Research question and hypothesis

What question does the paper ask? What do the authors claim, in one or two sentences?

## Background and related work

What prior work does it build on or argue against? Which references should I read next?

## Methodology and experimental setup

Models, data, training details, baselines, metrics, number of seeds, compute.

## Main findings

The key results, with numbers. Label each with its strength: established / empirical finding / interpretation (see [claim labels](../README.md#claim-labels)).

## Limitations and assumptions

Those the authors state, and those they do not.

## Reproducibility considerations

Is code released? Are hyperparameters and seeds reported? Could I reproduce a scaled-down version with my hardware?

## My critical analysis

- Does the evidence support the claim at the scope it is stated?
- Are the baselines tuned with equal effort?
- Are uncertainty estimates reported?
- What alternative explanations fit the results?
- For interpretability papers: which level of the [evidence ladder](../roadmap/06-model-interpretability.md#46-observation-versus-intervention) does each claim reach?
